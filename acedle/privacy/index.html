ACEdle Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

ACEdle is a small fan game made by ACE Clan, a group of friends. We collect as little as we can, we never sell or share it for advertising, and we have no ads or trackers.

On the website

  • Your browser keeps today's progress, your streaks and the name you typed (in local storage). It stays on your device; clear your browser data to remove it.
  • The leaderboard: if you press "Add my score", the name you typed and that day's results (guesses and points for each stage) are sent to our leaderboard server and shown to everyone on the leaderboard. They are kept so the all-time board works.

In Discord (the ACEdle Activity)

  • Discord sign-in: when you open ACEdle in a voice call, Discord asks you to allow it. We only ask for the identify permission, which gives us your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar. We don't get your email, messages, servers or friends list.
  • The call panel: your display name, avatar and progress (which stages you finished, in how many guesses, and your points) are shared with the other people playing in the same call. Your guesses and answers are never shared.
  • How long: this lives only in the games server's memory. It is wiped when the call's room has been idle for 6 hours or the server restarts. Discord's access token is passed back to your Discord app and is not saved on our server.
  • Logs: the games server's window shows a line with your display name when you sign in. It isn't saved to a file and disappears when the window closes.
  • The leaderboard works the same as on the website, with your Discord display name filled in for you. You can change it before adding your score.

Where it's kept and who handles it

  • The games server and leaderboard run on a clan member's computer in Australia, reached through Cloudflare.
  • In Discord, all traffic goes through Discord's own proxy. Discord's handling of your data is covered by Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Game pictures and data load from Riot Games' Data Dragon and from valorant-api.com, which see a normal web request (through Discord's proxy when in Discord).

Getting your data deleted

Message an ACE Clan admin in the ACE Clan Discord server, or reach us through aceclan.lol, and tell us the leaderboard name or Discord account. We'll remove your leaderboard entries. Anything from the call panel is already gone once the room closes. You can also remove ACEdle's access at any time in Discord under User Settings > Authorized Apps.

Children

ACEdle is for people old enough to use Discord under its own rules. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
