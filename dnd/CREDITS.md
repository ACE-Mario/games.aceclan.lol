## Token pictures found online

"Find one online" on a token searches Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org). Each result shows its
author and licence. Pictures there are free to use under their own licences (often public domain or Creative
Commons); credit the author if the licence asks for it.

## Token icons

Tokens without a picture show an icon from **game-icons.net** (https://game-icons.net), picked from a word in the
token's name (goblin, wolf, priest...) or chosen on the token card. The icons are by Lorc, Delapouite and the other
game-icons.net contributors, under **CC BY 3.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). They are copied
into `icons.js` (from the @iconify-json/game-icons package) as SVG paths, coloured to fit each token.
