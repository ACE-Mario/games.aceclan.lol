Campaigns

    What's in it
    • Maps: the DM adds a map picture (or uses a blank grid), lines the grid up with it and picks which scene the party sees.
    • Tokens: drag them square to square. Players move their own; the DM moves everything. Click one for HP, AC and conditions; Shift+click or Shift+drag picks several to move or damage together. The DM has ready-made monsters.
    • Fog of war: the DM paints squares in or out, or lets it clear by itself as the party explores. Monsters under the fog aren't sent to players at all.
    • Walls and doors: the DM traces the map's walls; sight stops at them. Anyone can open a door (unless the DM locked it).
    • Ruler, drawings and pings: measure moves, draw spell areas (circles, cones, squares, lines) and ping a spot so everyone looks.
    • Dice: rolled on the server, so nobody can fake one. Type 2d6+3, 1d20+5 adv or 4d6kh3, or roll straight from a character sheet. Rolls can go to the DM only.
    • Turn order: roll initiative for tokens and step through the rounds.
    • Character sheets: abilities, saves, skills, HP, attacks, spell slots, rests and gear. Click anything to roll it.
    • Notes: the DM's private notes, handouts with pictures the DM can show on everyone's screen, and your own notes.

    Moving around: drag empty space or use the middle mouse button, and the scroll wheel zooms. On a phone, drag with one finger and pinch to zoom.
