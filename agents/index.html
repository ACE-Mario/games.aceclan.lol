    How to play
    1. Split into Red and Purple. Each team picks one spymaster.
    2. One person shares this board in Discord. Send each spymaster the spymaster link, which shows the key: which words belong to which team.
    3. On your turn, your spymaster gives a one-word clue and a number, like Ocean 2. The number is how many cards it points at.
    4. Your team guesses by calling a card (for example "B3"), and the board host taps it. You get the number plus one guesses.
    5. Your colour: keep guessing. Grey bystander or the other team's card: your turn ends. The bomb: your team loses on the spot.
    6. The first team to find all its cards wins. The team that goes first has 9 cards, the other has 8.

    Clues can't be a word on the board or part of one. A clue of 0 or ∞ means guess as many as you like.
