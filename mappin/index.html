ACE Map Pin

Drop a pin where you think it is. Five VALORANT callouts and five Summoner's Rift spots a day. How close can you get? New maps at midnight (Adelaide time).

How to play
  • Tap the map to drop your pin. Tap again to move it, then Lock in.
  • After each pin you'll see the right spot and how close you were.
  • On Summoner's Rift, Blue side is the team in the bottom-left corner, Red side the top-right.
  • VALORANT callouts are the ones the game itself uses.
