How to play
  • Tap four tiles you think share something, then Submit.
  • Right: that squad is revealed in its colour. Gold is the easiest, then teal, purple, and red is the hardest.
  • "One away…" means three of your four belong together.
  • Watch out for red herrings: a tile can look like it fits two squads, but it only belongs to one.
  • When you finish, add your score to the clan leaderboard: 100 points for solving it, minus 20 for each mistake. If you run out of tries, 10 for each squad you found.
