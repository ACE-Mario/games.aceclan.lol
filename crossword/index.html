How to play
  • Tap a square and type. Tap the same square again, or use the clue lists, to switch between across and down.
  • Arrow keys move around; Backspace goes back a square.
  • Check marks any wrong letters, and Reveal fills in the square you're on. Both are counted in your result.
  • When you finish, add your time to the clan leaderboard. Each Check adds 10 seconds to your leaderboard time and each revealed square 30. All time adds up points: 100 for a minute or less, then a point less every 6 seconds (never under 10).
