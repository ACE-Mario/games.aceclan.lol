ACE Board

A shared whiteboard for ideas, plans and studying. Type anywhere, draw with the pen and paste pictures, and everyone in the room sees it live.

How it works
  • Text: pick the T tool and click anywhere to start typing.
  • Pen: draw freehand in any colour and size.
  • Pictures: press Ctrl+V to paste one, drag one onto the board, or use Add picture.
  • Move: with the arrow tool, drag your own things around, or drag empty space to look around the board. It goes on forever. Double-click your text to change it.
  • Undo: Ctrl+Z undoes your last change, Ctrl+Y puts it back.
  • Delete: select your thing and press Delete, or rub it out with the eraser.
  • The eye next to each player hides or shows their writing, just on your screen.

Boards stay open while people use them and close after 6 quiet hours. Nothing is saved once the room closes, so screenshot anything you want to keep.
