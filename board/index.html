    How it works
    • Text: pick the T tool and click anywhere to start typing.
    • Pen: draw freehand in any colour and size.
    • Pictures: press Ctrl+V to paste one, drag one onto the board, or use Add picture.
    • Documents: drop a PDF or Word (.docx) file on the board, or use Add picture. Its pages appear for everyone, so you can write, highlight and draw on them together.
    • Move: with the arrow tool, drag your own things around. To look around the board, middle-click and drag, hold Space and drag, or use one finger on a phone. It goes on forever. Double-click your text to change it.
    • Zoom: the scroll wheel zooms in and out. The target button takes you back to the middle.
    • Shapes and notes: boxes, circles, lines and arrows for diagrams, sticky notes for ideas, and a highlighter for marking things up.
    • Select several: drag a box around your things, or Shift+click. Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copies them, Ctrl+D duplicates.
    • Undo: Ctrl+Z undoes your last change, Ctrl+Y puts it back.
    • Delete: select your things and press Delete (or the bin button), or rub them out with the eraser. The leader can delete anyone's.
    • The eye next to each player hides or shows their writing, just on your screen.

    Every board is saved on the ACE server and listed here, so anyone can open it again while the server is running. Download picture saves a copy for yourself.
